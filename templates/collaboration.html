🧠 Glioma Brain Tumor - Patient Information
📋 What are Gliomas?

Gliomas are tumors arising from glial cells in the brain and spinal cord. They represent ~30% of all brain tumors and are classified by cell type and malignancy grade (I-IV). Early detection improves prognosis significantly.

⚠️ Glioma Classification Scale
Grade I:Low-grade, slow-growing, pilocytic astrocytoma
Grade II:Low-grade diffuse gliomas (astrocytoma, oligodendroglioma)
Grade III:High-grade, anaplastic tumors
Grade IV:Glioblastoma - most aggressive
No Tumor:Healthy brain tissue
💊 Evidence-Based Treatment Options
⚔️ Surgical Intervention
  • • Maximal safe resection preferred
  • • Extent of resection correlates with survival
  • • Awake surgery for eloquent areas
  • • Laser interstitial thermal therapy alternative
☢️ Radiation Therapy
  • • Standard: 54-60 Gy in 30 fractions
  • • Hypofractionated: 34 Gy in 10 fractions (GBM)
  • • Stereotactic radiosurgery for small tumors
  • • Proton therapy when available
💉 Systemic Therapy
  • • TMZ chemotherapy (standard for GBM)
  • • PCV regimen for oligodendrogliomas
  • • Targeted therapies based on molecular profiling
  • • Tumor-treating fields (Optune)
🔄 Alternative & Emerging Methods
💉 Immunotherapy Approaches
